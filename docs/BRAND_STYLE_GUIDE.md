# MyNigeriaGuide Brand & Product Style Guide\n\n## Brand position\n\nMyNigeriaGuide is an independent Nigerian consumer-information product that helps people complete public-service tasks with less confusion. It must feel trustworthy and premium without looking like an official government website.\n\n**Brand promise:** Clear steps. Verified sources.\n\n**Voice:** calm, direct, practical, transparent. Never bureaucratic, sensational or salesy.\n\n## Visual identity\n\n- Primary green: `#063F2D` — trust, navigation, high-emphasis actions.\n- Action green: `#087044` — links, focus and interactive emphasis.\n- Ivory: `#F8F5ED` — primary page canvas; warmer and more premium than pure white.\n- Surface: `#FFFDF8` — cards and elevated content.\n- Gold: `#D1A24A` — restrained brand accent only; never dominant.\n- Ink: `#10251C` — headings and high-contrast copy.\n- Muted: `#607168` — supporting text.\n- Line: `#D9E2DC` — borders and separators.\n\nGold is an accent, not a background theme. Green carries trust; ivory carries warmth.\n\n## Logo\n\nThe mark uses a **route line, starting waypoint, verified destination pin and small navigation star**. It deliberately contains no letter monogram, government seal, coat of arms or agency logo. The idea is simple: start here, follow a clear route, arrive at a verified official destination.\n\nRules:\n- Keep the mark on dark green.\n- Use the full `MyNigeriaGuide` wordmark in the main header and footer.\n- Do not imitate a federal or state seal.\n- Do not place agency logos beside the brand in a way that implies endorsement.\n\n## Typography\n\nUse the system sans stack for performance and reliability. Headlines use tight tracking and strong weight; body copy stays highly readable.\n\n- Hero: large, compact, high contrast.\n- H2: concise section statement, not a paragraph.\n- Body: 16px+ equivalent with generous line height.\n- Eyebrows: uppercase, small, letter-spaced; use sparingly.\n\n## Radius & depth\n\n- Core cards: 18–22px radius.\n- Controls: 11–14px radius.\n- Pills: fully rounded.\n- Shadows must be soft and low-opacity; borders remain visible.\n- Avoid excessive floating/glass effects.\n\n## Layout\n\n- Desktop max content width: ~1160px.\n- Reading column: ~760–820px.\n- Section spacing: 64–88px desktop, 44–56px mobile.\n- Important action/fee panels may use dark green to create hierarchy.\n- Mobile must never rely on squeezed desktop navigation; use a deliberate menu.\n\n## Service-page hierarchy\n\nEvery guide should reveal information in this order:\n\n1. What the service is and who owns it.\n2. Current fee/status and official portal.\n3. Online vs physical route.\n4. Requirements.\n5. Numbered steps.\n6. What happens after submission.\n7. Notes and FAQs.\n8. Official sources.\n\n## Trust presentation\n\nTrust must be earned visually through traceability, not by pretending to be official.\n\n- Always show verification status and last-checked date.\n- Keep responsible agency visible.\n- Keep official sources easy to open.\n- Never collect government application fees.\n- If official pages genuinely disagree, prefer the current purpose-built source when its role/date is clear and note the older discrepancy calmly.\n\n## Icons\n\nUse one consistent line-icon family with rounded strokes. Category icons are custom in-app vectors. Do not mix emojis, filled clip-art and unrelated icon sets.\n\n## Motion\n\nMotion should confirm interaction, not decorate the page. Use 140–220ms hover/press transitions and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.\n\n## Accessibility\n\n- Minimum touch target: 44px.\n- Strong focus ring on all interactive controls.\n- Keep WCAG AA contrast for body copy and controls.\n- Do not encode verification state by colour alone.\n- Maintain keyboard-operable navigation and skip-to-content.\n\n## Writing rules\n\nPrefer: “Continue on official portal”, “What you need”, “What happens next?”, “Checked 28 September 2026”.\n\nAvoid: bureaucratic filler, fake urgency, unsupported timelines, unofficial-agent recommendations, and language that implies MyNigeriaGuide is a government authority.\n\n## Premium test\n\nA new screen is on-brand only if it is: clear in five seconds, source-transparent, mobile-comfortable, visually calm, and consistent with the green/ivory/gold system.
