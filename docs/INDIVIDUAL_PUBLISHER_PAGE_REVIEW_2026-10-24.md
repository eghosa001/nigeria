- 695 raw service JSON records across 16 files were scanned for repeated complete text fields longer than 80 characters **within each record**. Zero exact long-field duplicates were found. These are raw records, not a verified count of unique public indexable URLs.
- For 659 records in the first seven files, a sentence-level scan found only three long passages shared by two different pages each: FRSC licence fee bands, CAC company-conversion processing time and NERC complaint escalation. They express legitimately shared issuer facts; similarity alone is **not** proof of scraped or low-value content.
- [PR #692](https://github.com/eghosa001/nigeria/pull/692) extended the site's **read-only** repetition auditor to also flag long exact normalized paragraphs **between distinct canonical URLs**. This yields a separate editorial evidence queue; no duplicate finding automatically removes, rewrites or noindexes any page.
- The source-level screening does **not** clear paraphrased duplication, copied third-party source prose, media-rights claims or all HTML/Javascript sections on 695 rendered pages. No mass deletion or padding is justified by these results.
