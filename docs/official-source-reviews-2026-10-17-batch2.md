# Government-source review — 17 October 2026, batch 2

Eight existing guides were checked against their issuing authorities' published information. The detailed checks were of: Nigeria e-Visa, Nigeria R10 Temporary Work Permit, US B1/B2, Germany Schengen, Netherlands Schengen, WAEC Second Series 2026, NECO 2026, and JAMB admission condonement.

Evidence by guide:
- [NIS official service directory](https://immigration.gov.ng/info-center/), [e-Visa FAQ](https://immigration.gov.ng/?echo_kb_faq=how-do-i-apply-for-an-e-visa), [dedicated eVisa portal](https://evisa.immigration.gov.ng/), [R10 details](https://immigration.gov.ng/info-center/temporary-work-permit-twp-visa-r10a/). Corrected previously misleading regular-visa portal URL and clarified specialist approval prerequisites. The separate TWP portal's generic wording is not substituted for the R10-specific rules.
- [US State visitor visa](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/tourism-visit/visitor.html), [visa-fee table](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/visa-information-resources/fees/fees-visa-services.html), [August 2026 Africa hub notice](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/News/visas-news/realignment-of-us-visa-services-in-africa-to-regional-hubs.html). Retained the published US$185 B visa fee; explained Abuja realignment and Lagos hub.
- [German Schengen guidance](https://nigeria.diplo.de/ng-en/2746378-2746378), [tourism checklist](https://nigeria.diplo.de/ng-en/2753074-2753074), [fee schedule](https://nigeria.diplo.de/ng-en/2746334-2746334), [remonstration decision](https://nigeria.diplo.de/ng-en/2716462-2716462). Documented the 2025 discontinuation of voluntary visa refusal remonstration.
- [Netherlands Nigerian submission routing](https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/visa-the-netherlands/schengen-visa/apply-nigeria), [tourism checklist](https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/visa-the-netherlands/checklist-schengen-visa-tourism), [Dutch processing times](https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/visa-the-netherlands/waiting-time-after-visa-application). The state-by-state distinction between Belgian TLScontact Abuja and French TLScontact Lagos is now explicit.
- [WAEC final 2026 Second Series notice](https://www.waecnigeria.org/index.php/article/wassce-private-candidates-2026-second-series-final-timetable). The actual attached PDF is the authority for individual paper times.
- [NECO 2026 notice](https://neco.gov.ng/?page_id=27), [external SSCE registration](https://ssceexternal.neco.gov.ng/), [external timetable PDF](https://neco.gov.ng/2026%20SSCE%20EXTERNAL%20TIMETABLE.pdf), [internal timetable PDF](https://neco.gov.ng/2026%20SSCE%20INTERNAL%20TIMETABLE-2.pdf). Added the official 26 October normal registration deadline and subsequent NGN 5000 late fee, distinctly from examination dates.
- [JAMB published payment and service menu](https://jamb.gov.ng/Payment-Services), [JAMB portal](https://portal.jamb.gov.ng/). Clarified separate condonement fee and centre charge.

Only these eight older guides and their referenced source-review dates were refreshed. Where the online result is an indexed publisher snapshot, this is a desk verification of published official guidance, not a real-time portal transaction or appointment check. Other older guides retain their original dates, including NELFUND and INEC. The scheduled six-hour improvement cycle already covers additional source verification.
