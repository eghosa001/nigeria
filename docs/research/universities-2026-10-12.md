Research sources are linked **inside each university record**. Closed windows are explicitly past. Personal PINs, JAMB details, payment and clearance remain on official institution/JAMB portals. The pages are distinct from the general JAMB CAPS and NELFUND Services guides. No scraper, unofficial university database, transfer of funds, exam-bypass workflow or false live admission promises.
