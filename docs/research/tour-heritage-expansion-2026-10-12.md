# Official-source Tour heritage expansion — 12 October 2026

## Editorial scope

Twelve **non-indexable place records**, all attached to existing canonical Tour guides; one existing Aba museum entry corrected; answer-first improvements to the existing Kogi, Bayelsa and Uyo destination pages. This is not an invitation to generate twelve competing new SEO URLs. Parent guides, gallery sources and internal search already provide discovery.

## Evidence checked

- National Commission for Museums and Monuments: https://museum.ng/museums/national-museums/ — city, address and operational identity for federal museums in Uyo, Yenagoa, Lokoja, Port Harcourt, Sokoto, Oyo and Aba. NCMM is not an assurance of today's exhibits, prices, opening hours or unobstructed access.
- Kogi State Government visitor directory: https://kogistate.gov.ng/kogi-state-where-rivers-kiss-the-sky/ — royal palaces in Okene and Idah, Inike Lake and Ajaokuta Steel Complex; ordinary state tourism promotion is not authorisation to enter a royal court or industrial plant.
- Kogi State development plan: https://mainbackup.kogistate.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/Kogi-State-Development-Plan-2024-2056.pdf — World War II cenotaph and Royal Niger flag-post in Lokoja; exact visitor entrance cannot be inferred from a planning document.
- Bayelsa Tourism: https://motd.bayelsastate.gov.ng/tourism-sites/ — oil heritage and the proposed Oloibiri museum. Keep the proposed project distinct from the NCMM-listed National Museum Yenagoa.

## Publication decision and caveats

No verified venue opening, ticket fee, live tour, guide availability, access conditions, camera permission, boat or swimming service was claimed. Especially for Ajaokuta, royalty sites and unpinned monuments, warn readers to confirm access rather than offering booking or automatic door-to-door directions. Source dates correspond to this editorial review, not a claim of a personal site visit.
