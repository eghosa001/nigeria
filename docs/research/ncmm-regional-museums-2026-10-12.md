# NCMM regional museums / Adamawa heritage additions — 12 October 2026

## Individually verified listings

Ten new, non-indexable destination records using the Nigerian National Commission for Museums and Monuments official museum directory: https://museum.ng/museums/national-museums/.

- **Adamawa**: National Museum Yola (Mohammed Tukur Road, Jimeta), National Museum Fombina (Lamido Palace, Jimeta), National Museum Hong (Hong Local Government Secretariat), Sukur Interpretation Centre (Mubi–Maiduguri Road, Madagali). A distinct source-backed **Adamawa museum itinerary** groups the first two as Yola/Jimeta city stops and flags Hong and Sukur as separate journeys. Sukur is not marketed as a guaranteed safe day trip.
- **Borno**: National Museum Maiduguri, Custom Area (check security and admission).
- **Niger**: National Museum Minna, Federal Secretariat (government-premises access not guaranteed).
- **Gombe**: National Museum Gombe, second floor of the Federal Secretariat (not an assumed open public park).
- **Osun**: National Museum Osogbo, Ataoja's Palace (museum distinct from palace protocol but shared precinct).
- **Jigawa**: National Museum Birnin Kudu, Kano–Bauchi Road (specific heritage institution, distinct from town-level destination).
- **Plateau**: Centre for Earth Construction Technology (CECTECH), Jos (specialist NCMM venue, opposite Jos Museum).

The commission's directory provides addresses, NOT real-time accessibility, ticket prices, safe road conditions, disability facilities or confirmation of exhibitions. Records explicitly warn readers to confirm rather than claim they attended. Source directory checked 12 October 2026.
