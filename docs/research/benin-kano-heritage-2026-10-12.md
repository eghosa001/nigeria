# Benin City and Kano heritage-depth review — 12 October 2026

**Canonical intent:** strengthen existing `/explore/benin-city` and `/explore/kano` without creating city-name-variant doorway pages. Both already contain a working museum place listing, and their canonical city guides already have itinerary-like planning sections. Seven additional place directory records serve distinct existing, named heritage sites.

**Benin evidence:** Edo State Government identifies the royal palace, Emotan Statue, Ogba Zoo and Great Benin Moat as destinations: https://edostate.gov.ng/visit-edo-state-to-enjoy-her-unique-hospitality-and-tourism-signature/. The Edo State Tourism Agency specifically places the Oba's Palace on Plymouth Road: https://tourism.edostate.gov.ng/the-royal-palace-of-the-oba-of-benin/. NCMM includes Benin City Walls and Moat in its national-monuments list: https://museum.ng/national-monuments-in-nigeria/list-of-national-monuments/. The public statue is opposite Oba Market; there is no claim of a staffed entrance.

**Duplicate control:** An existing Kofar Mata dye-pits listing was detected by the Tour content validation check; it is retained instead of cloning it. The canonical Kano guide now also highlights that existing listing.

**Kano evidence:** UNESCO's 2007 *Tentative List* entry, submitted by Nigeria's NCMM, covers the city walls and gates, Kurmi Market and Emir's Palace; importantly, tentative-list status is **not** UNESCO World Heritage inscription: https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/5171/. The Nigerian Tourism Development Authority profiles Kofar Mata's dyeing tradition: https://tournigeria.gov.ng/home-to-africas-oldest-dye-pit/.

**Editorial decisions:** Public access, present visitor operations, dates, hours, prices, park animal inventory and current area safety have not been independently verified. Do not infer any from the existence of an official older listing. Palace interiors and craft workplaces require permission; earthworks and city walls occupy **multiple sections**, so no false single-ticket address or GPS point is asserted. No current security or transport guarantee was made. Existing museums, Igun Street, Dala Hill, and canonical city URLs are retained, not duplicated.

**Search/discovery:** Records are attached to existing city guides; distinct names and descriptive tags feed existing Tour discovery. No standalone indexable place articles or new sitemap URLs are created. New text is reader-specific and answer-first; source/date metadata stays in the existing record fields.

**Validation:** Short record-level invariant test, review integration imports and existing city guide references. No irrelevant project-wide tests or deployment runs.
