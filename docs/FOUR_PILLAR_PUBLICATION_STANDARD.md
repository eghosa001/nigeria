# MyNigeriaGuide: four-pillar publication standard
Version: 2026-10-15. Applies to new pages, changed pages and the existing backlog.
This is an **internal editorial threshold**, not a claim that Google certifies a
specific word count, score or schema. Google's review decision is independent.

## All four pillars — non-negotiable prepublication gates
1. **Reader intent and original usefulness.** Answer the main query immediately.
   Add independently reasoned advice or practical work that is not simply a
   rewritten company description, scraped snippet, translated publisher copy,
   list of links, or autogenerated FAQ. Remove repeated paragraphs. Only publish
   if there is a reason for someone to choose this page over the source alone.
2. **Claims and primary evidence.** Check each material claim against an
   identified, current original issuer/publisher/operator. Include source links,
   checked dates and honest uncertainty. A single original primary source may
   suffice for a narrowly factual claim; competing sources, high-stakes facts,
   controversial issues or changing availability require corroboration.
   User-generated reports, Reddit, TikTok, X, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube
   comments and forum posts can expose real usability problems and omissions,
   but must be labeled anecdotal and checked independently before being
   written as general facts. Never fabricate a test, visit, quote or interview.
3. **Links and transactions.** Open the actual source and final destination,
   not only its homepage. Confirm working status, appropriate target and current
   action where reasonably possible. Redirects, CAPTCHAs and logins are
   **inconclusive**, not evidence that a form works. Never replace a source
   solely because an automated checker cannot reach it. Provide a safe
   alternative and review date when validation is blocked.
4. **Structured mobile-first experience.** One clear title, direct answer,
   useful factual sections, relevant quick facts, descriptive headings,
   working internal navigation, accessible controls, image alt/rights, sane
   spacing, no mobile overflow, and an obvious official next step. Lists and
   schema must match what readers can see. Broken/expired links and misleading
   dates are blockers; ads never obscure content. Do not inject lengthy
   boilerplate to hit a word target.
5. **Trust, indexing and duplication.** Visible About, Contact, Privacy,
   Editorial, Correction and disclosure pages. Correct canonical and robot
   directives. Consolidate substantially equivalent pages; keep low-value
   navigation/search/filter archives usable but noindex when appropriate.
   No AdSense placements on copied/empty/directory-only or under-construction
   screens. Sitemaps include only canonical indexable URLs.
6. **Editorial signoff.** Record why the page is valuable, which claims were
   independently checked, gaps, copyright basis and any unresolved risks.
   Automated passing checks cannot certify truth, first-hand experience,
   user satisfaction, legal image rights or Google eligibility. No fake author,
   reviewer or "personally visited" claim. When facts are inconclusive, hold
   the relevant claim/page rather than invent it.
7. **Review cadence.** Open vacancies and fast-changing fees/closures demand
   short validity windows; evergreen explainers can use longer windows.
   Old dates are a recheck queue, not proof of an error. A meaningful update
   requires a real source check rather than bumping a timestamp.

## Movies and Entertainment: editorial film guide
- **Lead with:** actual title, production/year/format, genre/language (only
  verified), spoiler-light independently written story overview and source.
- **Credits:** verified principal cast, director/crew when available, and
  actor-to-character roles **only** with specific evidence. List unknown
  credits honestly; do not infer them from metadata or fill with false names.
- **Where to watch:** film-specific authorized publisher/distributor link,
  access method (free, subscription, cinema, pay-per-view), country/availability
  and check date; otherwise clearly say availability is unverified. A trailer
  is not full-film streaming evidence.
- **What adds value:** non-repeated film-specific context, relationships/themes,
  release history, important story questions, original commentary or
  comparisons sourced to specific facts. No generic plot padding.
- **Media and aliases:** credit and document rights to posters/frames;
  publisher thumbnails must point to the official source. Same film has one
  curated canonical. Directory-only YouTube records without editorial curation
  stay discoverable but should not be standalone indexable articles.
- **Validation:** cast/role accuracy, date/runtime, no invented ending, working
  watch link, exact film identity, no copied publisher synopsis.

## Services: task-completion guide
- **Direct answer:** who qualifies, which exact service, prerequisites,
  documents, fees and uncertainties, expected turnaround only if official.
- **Step-by-step:** official starting URL and precise ordered actions, what the
  applicant enters/uploads, when payments/appointments are needed, common
  mistakes, confirmation and what to do after applying. Distinguish online,
  in-person and hybrid processes without guessing.
- **Completion and recovery:** how to track, collect, correct, appeal or reach
  official support; warnings for scams, unofficial fees and sensitive data.
- **Multiple perspectives:** check relevant official agency pages and credible
  social/user problem reports; add commonly observed friction only if
  corroborated and appropriately attributed. Never label anecdotes guaranteed.
- **Categorisation:** accurate agency, category, intent and meaningfully related
  next guides; no dead ends or self-links.
- **Validation:** direct portal and supporting links tested, key fees and
  deadlines current, source/last-checked date, claims matched to service type.

## Tour Nigeria: genuinely useful trip planning
- **Why go:** location context and distinctive, verifiable heritage, culture,
  landscape, food, architecture or activities; explain who will enjoy it and
  who might not. Avoid interchangeable 'beautiful place' sales language.
- **Logistics:** realistic route and entry point, transport options, practical
  travel time *only if sourced or explicitly estimated*, season/weather
  caveats, opening/access status, official booking, costs where published,
  accessibility, safety and local etiquette. Flag uncertain costs/hours.
- **Experience:** a sourced firsthand account when actually obtained with
  permission, or clearly attributed visitor perspective; never invent an
  eyewitness review. Original comparison and route trade-offs can add value.
- **Itinerary:** credible half-day/day plan, nearby stops, what to prioritise,
  timing/dependencies and alternatives when access is limited.
- **Validation:** verify individual place identity and map/official source;
  distinguish active attractions from nearby landmarks and closed facilities.
  Avoid duplicating near-identical state/city pages solely for more URLs.
- **No place-card quotas or misleading proximity:** an archaeological artefact,
  sacred location or heritage site may have one properly sourced reference or
  none, not three unrelated state attractions added to fill a layout. State
  clearly whether a map refers to the historical findspot, preservation site,
  visitor entrance or an approximate area; do not imply any is the same place.
  Map links and a historic tourism directory are not evidence that a venue is
  open, safe, priced or conveniently reachable. For regions under current
  do-not-travel warnings, label mapping as research-only and avoid presenting
  suggested local excursions or bookable itineraries.

## Jobs and Careers: verified application pathways
- **Strict types:** individual live vacancy; recruitment programme; employer
  career portal; screening/ongoing process; expired/closed archive; evergreen
  career advice. A career portal is not an 'open job'.
- **Open:** only if an official employer/agency notice or direct valid listing
  supports an active application window, applicant eligibility and application
  route. Show employer, specific role, location, contract, salary only if
  stated, date posted, deadline/time zone when published, last checked and
  exact official Apply link. 'Open' is never inferred solely from an old record.
- **Apply instructions:** eligibility, documents, concrete submission steps,
  confirmation/next stage and anti-scam cautions. Never accept payments for
  access or claim an interview/shortlist can be bought.
- **Closed/uncertain:** elapsed deadlines cannot appear in 'open now' or
  live JobPosting schema; keep useful closures/screening records categorized
  separately. Unverified/broken destination => **status unverified** and do
  not claim accepting applications until issuer confirms.
- **Verification:** use employer's own careers/ATS notice first. Independent
  professional/social reports can surface scams or practical details but
  cannot independently reopen a job. Recheck fast-changing vacancies.

## Audit and release decision
The automated full-catalog report classifies each page by:
- **BLOCKER:** verifiable data/structure contradiction or missing essential
  user pathway. Do not newly publish or index; fix, redirect or safely noindex.
- **EDITORIAL REVIEW:** plausible concern (weak originality, limited narrative
  evidence, repeated content or stale claims) requiring individual checks.
- **MANUAL CHECK:** link reachability, source truth, rights, mobile feel,
  visitor experience and creator permissions cannot be guaranteed by the
  repository. Track explicitly, do not silently mark passed.
- **READY FOR HUMAN SIGNOFF:** automatic checks passed; still not a Google
  approval certificate.

For existing pages, do a **complete inventory** and remediate priority failures
in descending order of harm, user interest and search impressions. Preserve the
working experience and avoid blanket deletions based on word count. New records
must meet stricter gates before being promoted to standalone indexable content.
Automated reports use all imported catalog rows; live HTTP/browser checks are a
separate, sampled validation step and must not be represented as a full crawl.
